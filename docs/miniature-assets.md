# Miniature accents

The gallery attendant is an AI-generated interpretation of the user-supplied Persian miniature reference, created with the built-in image generation tool. It is not a literal historical cutout. Its transparent PNG is stored at `assets/miniature-attendant.png`.

The bird is a real-time, procedural Three.js model in `miniature-bird.js`, with layered feathers and gold details. Drag it or use arrow keys while focused; Home resets its pose. Subtle head movement respects reduced-motion preferences.

## Image-generation prompt

Use case: background-extraction and precise-object-edit. Asset: one transparent PNG cutout for a portfolio website, not a whole scene. Input image is the supplied Persian miniature reference. Isolate and carefully recreate ONLY the standing blue-robed attendant at the bottom right holding a platter of fruit: white turban, blue robe with tiny gold floral motifs, pale stockings, small black shoes, body turned left. Preserve the delicate hand-painted Persian miniature style, aged pigments and fine outlines from the source. Change the arms/pose slightly so BOTH hands hold a slim level empty gold tray horizontally just above shoulder height, projecting to the LEFT; this tray will visually support the bottom edge of a website project card. Full body visible from turban to shoes. Keep figure slender, graceful, 16th century miniature proportions, not cartoon, not photorealistic. Subject centered, with generous room for the projecting tray. Genuine transparent background (alpha), no checkerboard pixels, no white rectangle, no architecture, no border, no other people, no shadows on a background, no text or calligraphy. Tight composition around the figure with 5% transparent margin. Portrait output.
